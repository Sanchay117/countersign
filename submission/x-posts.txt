Countersign: the second signature on every AI agent payment.

A spending limit knows how much. It can't know what for.

Limits alone stopped 5 of 40 bad payments. Countersign stopped all 40.

Built on SERV Reasoning + Kite for the @openservai hackathon.

https://countersign-ten-xi.vercel.app
---
How it reads a payment: 6 exact checks in code (free), then 5 judgment calls by SERV Reasoning on its smallest model, about a tenth of a cent.

Every run on the site is a real x402 payment on Kite testnet.

Code and the full trap suite: https://github.com/Sanchay117/countersign
